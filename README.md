# Dominator Pest Control Homepage Redesign

## 1. Website Overview

Dominator Pest Control is a Dubai-based pest management company providing residential, commercial, and industrial pest control, termite protection, bird control, and disinfection services.

The redesigned homepage focuses on communicating the company's complete service offering, making service discovery easier for different customer groups, and creating a more straightforward enquiry journey. The design prioritises clear business positioning, organised service categories, and accessible contact options.

## 2. Main Improvements

### 1. Stronger Homepage Positioning and First Impression
- Improved the homepage headline to communicate Dominator's pest management and disinfection services more clearly.
- Made the residential, commercial, and industrial customer segments easier to identify.
- Strengthened the presentation of the company's core services and business offering.
- Addressed the previous lack of clarity about what the company provides and whom it serves.

### 2. Simplified Service Navigation
- Organised individual pest control treatments into clearer service categories.
- Improved the distinction between residential, commercial, and industrial solutions.
- Made specialist services, including termite treatment, rodent control, bird management, and disinfection, easier to discover.
- Reduced the complexity of navigating numerous individual services.

### 3. Streamlined Enquiry and Conversion Journey
- Addressed unnecessary complexity caused by lengthy and duplicated enquiry forms.
- Improved the visibility of direct contact options.
- Prioritised telephone and WhatsApp enquiries alongside quotation requests.
- Created a more straightforward path for customers looking to discuss their pest control requirements.

## 3. Design Improvements

- **Visual presentation:** Improved the homepage content hierarchy to give greater prominence to the company's core services and business positioning.
- **Layout:** Organised service information into clearer categories for residential, commercial, and industrial customers.
- **User experience:** Simplified service discovery and reduced friction in the enquiry journey.
- **Consistency:** Established a more structured presentation of pest management services and contact options.
- **Overall appearance:** Created a cleaner, more organised professional homepage focused on communicating expertise and encouraging customer enquiries.

## Project Objective

The redesign addresses the three critical issues identified during the original website research: weak homepage positioning, cluttered service navigation, and a complicated enquiry process. The overall objective is to help customers understand Dominator's services quickly, find relevant pest management solutions, and contact the company with less effort.
